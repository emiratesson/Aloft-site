Aloft

Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

In short: Aloft has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter, and your location, stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

  • Your route and flight details (airports, flight time, stage timing) are saved on your iPhone so the app works in airplane mode.
  • Location (GPS), if you allow it, is used while a flight is active to show your aircraft on the map, follow your route and keep the flight stages in sync. Your location and the path flown are stored only on your iPhone, are never sent to us or anyone else, and are cleared when you start a new flight.
  • Motion sensors are read live for the Bump Meter. Nothing is recorded or sent.
  • Preferences such as breathing session length are stored on your iPhone.

We do not collect, receive or store any of this information. Deleting the app deletes it.

Services the app contacts

  • Aviation weather: to show airport weather and en-route warnings, the app downloads public reports from the NOAA Aviation Weather Center (aviationweather.gov). Requests contain only airport codes. Like any website, that service can see your device's IP address. No personal information is sent.
  • Maps: the live map uses Apple Maps, subject to Apple's privacy policy. The offline chart is built into the app.

Children

Aloft does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
