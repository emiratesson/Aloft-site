Aloft · your calm co-pilot

Every bump, explained.

Aloft walks nervous flyers through their flight, stage by stage — what you'll hear, what you'll feel, and why it's normal. It's written by an airline pilot and licensed aircraft engineer, and it works fully in airplane mode.

Your flight, stage by stage — swipe from boarding to landing; GPS keeps you in sync, even offline.

Weather through a pilot's eyes — live airport reports and official en-route warnings, explained in plain words.

Sounds, bumps and breathing — every clunk explained, a live bump meter, and guided breathing sessions.

Behind the scenes — the security, baggage and engineering checks that happen before you board.

Support

Aloft is currently in beta on TestFlight. Questions, ideas or problems? Email widekeys.support@gmail.com with "Aloft" in the subject, or send feedback straight from TestFlight by taking a screenshot in the app.

The map or position looks wrong? Open the Map, tap "Match the seatback screen" and enter the time or distance to destination shown on your seatback map.

No weather showing? Weather is downloaded before you fly. Add your route while you still have a connection — it's saved for airplane mode.

No GPS? GPS works in airplane mode but can be weaker inside the cabin. Aloft carries on from your last fix or your schedule.

Aloft is for reassurance and education only. It is not an operational or safety tool. Always follow the instructions of your crew.
